What stays the same for every business

  • Your own assistant. Each business gets its own independent WaziBot assistant, set up with your real products, prices and policies.
  • Human handoff. Routine questions are answered automatically; conversations that need a person are handed to you or your team.
  • One dashboard. Orders, customers, products and bookings are tracked in one place instead of in a chat thread.
