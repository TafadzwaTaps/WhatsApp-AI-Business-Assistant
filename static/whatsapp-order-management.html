WhatsApp Order Management

Taking orders through WhatsApp is easy to start and hard to scale — a handful of daily messages is manageable, but once orders start coming in from multiple customers at once, it's easy to lose track of who ordered what, whether they've paid, and what still needs to be fulfilled. WaziBot turns that WhatsApp conversation into a real, trackable order system.

The problem with managing orders in a chat thread

When every order lives inside a WhatsApp conversation, there's no single place to see what's pending, what's been paid, and what's ready to go out. Orders get missed between messages, payment confirmations get buried, and following up on an order from three days ago means scrolling back through unrelated chat history to find it.

How WaziBot handles WhatsApp orders

  • The AI builds the order as the customer describes what they want, confirming items and quantities before checkout.
  • Payment is collected inline — EcoCash, PayPal, cash on delivery or pickup, bank transfer, or BLIK, depending on what you accept.
  • Every order lands in your dashboard immediately, with a clear status — pending, confirmed, preparing, ready, delivered, or cancelled — so nothing depends on remembering a chat thread.
  • Customers get automatic status updates as their order moves through that lifecycle, without you having to message them yourself.
  • Stock levels update in real time, so an item that's sold out stops being offered before a customer tries to order it.

Order tracking for the customer, too

A customer can check on their own order at any time by referencing their order number directly in WhatsApp — no separate tracking website or app required.

Built for real order volume

As order volume grows, the same system keeps working — every order is a real record in your dashboard, not just a message in a thread, so the process doesn't break down as your business gets busier.
