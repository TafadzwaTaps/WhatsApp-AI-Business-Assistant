WhatsApp Marketing

WhatsApp marketing means reaching your existing customers directly where they already talk to you — not building a separate email list or hoping they see a social media post. WaziBot gives small businesses three practical ways to market through WhatsApp: broadcast campaigns, QR codes, and referrals.

Broadcast campaigns

Send a message to your customer list — a new product, a promotion, a reminder that you're open for the holidays — directly to the same WhatsApp conversation they already order through. There's no separate app for customers to check; the message lands exactly where they're already paying attention.

QR codes that go straight to WhatsApp or booking

Generate a QR code that opens directly into a chat with your business, or straight to your public booking page if you take appointments. Print it on a flyer, a receipt, a table tent, or a business card — anyone who scans it lands in a live conversation or booking flow immediately, with no extra step.

Referrals

WaziBot includes a built-in referral program — customers or business owners can share a personal referral link or a branded, shareable QR code, and signups through it are tracked automatically.

Marketing that doesn't need a separate tool

Because campaigns, QR codes, and referrals all live inside the same WaziBot dashboard you already use for orders and bookings, there's no separate marketing platform to learn or pay for on top of everything else.
