Inventory Management

WhatsApp Inventory Management

Selling through WhatsApp without real stock tracking leads to a familiar problem: a customer orders something that's already sold out, and now you're apologizing and refunding instead of confirming a sale. WaziBot keeps your stock levels connected directly to the conversation your customers are ordering through.

How it works

You manage your product catalog and stock levels from your WaziBot dashboard. As orders come in through WhatsApp, stock updates in real time — so the AI assistant always knows exactly what's actually available before it confirms an order, not what was available when you last checked.

What this prevents

  • Overselling — an item that's out of stock stops being offered before a customer tries to order it, not after they've already paid.
  • Manual stock-checking — you don't need to pause and check a spreadsheet or a back room before confirming what you can sell.
  • Awkward refunds — customers don't get a confirmation for something you can't actually deliver.

Built for how small businesses actually track stock

Inventory management here isn't a separate, complex system — it's the same dashboard you already use for products, orders, and customers, with stock levels as one more field you keep current.

Not every business needs this — and that's fine

Service businesses booking appointments rather than selling physical stock can skip inventory tracking entirely; WaziBot adapts based on whether your business sells products, offers services, or both.
