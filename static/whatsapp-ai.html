WhatsApp AI

WhatsApp AI for Business

WhatsApp AI means putting an artificial intelligence assistant directly inside your business's WhatsApp conversations — so customer questions, orders, and bookings get handled automatically, without someone manually typing every reply. WaziBot is a WhatsApp AI built specifically for small businesses.

What "WhatsApp AI" actually means in practice

Most small businesses already run their sales through WhatsApp. A WhatsApp AI assistant sits inside that same conversation — the customer doesn't download a new app or learn a new interface, they message the business's existing WhatsApp number exactly as they always have. The AI reads what they're asking for, understands it, and responds using the business's real information: real products, real prices, real availability.

Why AI, specifically, and not just automated replies

Simple auto-reply tools can send a fixed message when someone texts a keyword, but they can't hold an actual conversation — a customer asking "do you have the blue one in stock?" or "can I come in at 3 instead of 2?" needs a system that understands the question, not just matches a keyword. WaziBot's AI is built to handle that kind of real, back-and-forth conversation, using the specific business's own catalog and rules rather than generic scripted answers.

What WaziBot's WhatsApp AI can do

  • Answer questions naturally — pricing, availability, hours, location — using the business's real, current information.
  • Take orders conversationally — building a cart and walking the customer through checkout inside the chat.
  • Handle appointment bookings — checking real availability and confirming a specific time slot.
  • Recognize when it should hand off — routing genuinely complex or sensitive conversations to a real person instead of guessing.

Who benefits most from WhatsApp AI

Businesses that get the most value are the ones already fielding a high volume of repetitive WhatsApp messages — the same handful of questions, over and over, at all hours — and losing time or losing sales because they can't answer fast enough, especially outside business hours.

Getting started with WhatsApp AI

With WaziBot, there's no WhatsApp Business API setup, developer, or technical configuration required to begin — a business can connect a number and start taking AI-assisted orders within minutes.
