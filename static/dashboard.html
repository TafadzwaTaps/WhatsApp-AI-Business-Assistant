ℹ️ How It Works

🛒 Cart Recovery — runs hourly. Customers idle for 60+ minutes receive a WhatsApp nudge linking back to their cart.

🔄 Re-engagement — runs daily. Customers with no orders in 14+ days receive a personalised "we miss you" message.

Both automations are opt-in and respect WhatsApp messaging policies. Messages are sent using your business name.

Danger Zone
Delete My Account
This closes your WaziBot account for good. Your login stops working immediately, but nothing is thrown away on the spot — we keep a backup of your account data (profile, products, orders, customers, chat history) for 90 days, then it is permanently deleted along with your account. You cannot undo this yourself once it's done — within the 90 days, only contacting WaziBot support can restore the account.
Delete My Account
Your account will be deactivated right away and you won't be able to log back in. A backup of your data (CSV/SQL) is kept for 90 days, then it and your account are permanently purged. There is no self-service recovery — if you change your mind, you must contact WaziBot support before the 90 days are up.
