WhatsApp Customer Support

Most customer support for small businesses already happens on WhatsApp, whether or not the business calls it "support" — it's the same messages, questions, and complaints that would go to a support inbox anywhere else. WaziBot automates the repetitive parts of that conversation while keeping a real person available for what actually needs one.

What slows support down on WhatsApp

The same handful of questions come up constantly — hours, location, pricing, order status — and answering each one individually, especially outside working hours, eats time that could go toward the business itself. Meanwhile, messages that genuinely need a human response can get buried behind routine questions, or answered hours later than they should be.

How WaziBot handles support

  • Answers routine questions instantly, at any hour, using the business's real, current information.
  • Checks order and booking status directly for customers who ask, without needing a human to look it up.
  • Recognizes when a conversation needs a person — a complaint, a refund request, something outside its own knowledge — and hands it off cleanly rather than guessing at an answer.
  • Keeps a record of every conversation in your dashboard, so nothing depends on remembering what was said in a chat from last week.

Human handoff, done properly

Automating support doesn't mean removing the option to talk to a real person. When WaziBot hands a conversation off, you or your team pick it up directly inside the same WhatsApp thread the customer is already in — there's no separate support tool for the customer to switch to.

Support that scales without a support team

For a small business, WaziBot's WhatsApp customer support means the routine 80% of questions get handled automatically, freeing up time to focus on the conversations that genuinely need a person's judgment.
