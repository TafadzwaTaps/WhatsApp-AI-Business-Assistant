WhatsApp Business Automation

WhatsApp business automation means letting a system — not a person typing every reply — carry your customer conversations from first message through to a completed order or booking. WaziBot automates that whole process for small businesses, without needing a developer or a WhatsApp Business API setup.

What actually gets automated

It's easy to think of automation as just auto-replies, but that only covers the first message. WaziBot automates the entire conversation: answering follow-up questions, building a cart, walking a customer through checkout, collecting payment, checking appointment availability, confirming a booking, and sending status updates as an order or booking moves forward — all without you manually typing a single reply.

What stays in your control

  • Your product catalog and pricing — WaziBot only ever quotes what you've actually set up in your dashboard.
  • Your payment methods — EcoCash, PayPal, cash, bank transfer, or BLIK, whichever you choose to accept.
  • Your working hours and availability — for businesses taking bookings, automation respects the hours you set, not a generic default.
  • When a human steps in — automation hands off to you directly for anything it shouldn't handle alone.

Why automate WhatsApp specifically

For most small businesses in Africa and increasingly in Europe, WhatsApp isn't a secondary channel — it's where the actual sales conversation happens. Automating it directly, rather than pushing customers to a separate website or app, means the automation lives exactly where your customers already are.

Getting started

Connect a WhatsApp number — shared or your own dedicated one — set up your products or services, and WaziBot's automation is live. Most businesses take their first automated order within minutes of signing up.
