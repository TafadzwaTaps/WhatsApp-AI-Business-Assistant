WhatsApp ordering for restaurants

Food orders often arrive as a stream of WhatsApp messages: what's on the menu, what's available today, how to pay. WaziBot answers those questions and turns the conversation into a tracked order.

How WaziBot helps a restaurant

  • Menu questions answered. The AI replies using your real products and prices, so customers can ask what's available or whether something fits their budget.
  • Orders built in the chat. A cart is built as the customer chooses items, confirmed before checkout, and created as an order in your dashboard straight away.
  • Payment your way. Accept the methods you configure: EcoCash, PayPal, cash on delivery or pickup, bank transfer or BLIK.
  • Status updates sent for you. Customers are updated automatically as an order moves from pending to confirmed, preparing, ready and delivered.
  • Out-of-stock items flagged early. If you track stock, a sold-out item is not offered as available.
  • A menu page and QR code. WaziBot can generate a public storefront for your business, and QR codes that open your WhatsApp chat.
  • A person when it matters. Complaints or unusual requests can be handed to you or your staff instead of forcing an AI answer.

Getting started

Add your menu items and prices as products, choose your payment methods, and connect WhatsApp. The free trial includes full access, so you can test the whole flow before choosing a plan.
