WhatsApp booking for salons and beauty businesses

Appointments usually get arranged by back-and-forth messages. WaziBot lets customers book through WhatsApp or a public booking page, checking your real availability before confirming.

How WaziBot helps a salon or beauty business

  • Booking through WhatsApp. Customers can ask for an appointment in a normal conversation, or use a public booking page.
  • Your real availability. WaziBot checks your working hours and existing bookings before confirming a slot, so two customers are not booked into the same appointment by accident.
  • Automatic reminders. Reminders are sent before the appointment, asking the customer to confirm or cancel.
  • Services and prices on hand. The assistant answers questions about what you offer and what it costs using the information you've added.
  • QR codes for walk-ins and posters. Generate a QR code that opens your WhatsApp chat or booking page.
  • A person when needed. Special requests can be handed to you or your team directly.
  • Customers and bookings in one place. Your dashboard shows bookings alongside customers and any product sales.

Getting started

Add your services, set your working hours and connect WhatsApp. The 30-day free trial includes full access so you can test booking end to end.
